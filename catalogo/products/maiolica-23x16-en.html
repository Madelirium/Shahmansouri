Details

This maiolica piece originates from Iran and has a rounded body with four necks: one upright and three angled, each with a circular opening. Its polychrome decoration combines stylised flowers, leaves and fruit, outlined in dark lines over a light base. Blue, green, yellow, pink and orange animate the design, while geometric bands in blue and light tones define the necks and lower section. The overall composition is dense and lively.
