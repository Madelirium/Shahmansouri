Details

This Qucian namakdan has a broad body and a narrow neck decorated on both sides. Small ivory and brown cross-shaped motifs form a diamond arrangement on the dark field around a pale central element. The concentric borders alternate toothed outlines, fine lines and angular red and blue forms on an ivory ground. The neck repeats the border design on a smaller scale. The back of the body is brick-red fabric, with sections of multicoloured edging along the sides.
